Drawverse 隱私政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 23 日 · 適用於 iOS App「Drawverse（畫到崩潰）」

一句話版本：Drawverse 不需要你註冊、不要你的姓名或 email、沒有廣告、不做追蹤、不賣資料。 我們只保存讓遊戲能運作的東西：一個匿名帳號編號、你取的暱稱、你畫的圖，以及基本的遊戲紀錄。 你隨時可以在 App 裡「設定 → 刪除我的資料」把這些全部刪掉。

一、我們蒐集什麼

1. 匿名帳號編號

你第一次開啟 App 時，系統會自動建立一個匿名帳號（不需要 email、電話或任何個人身分資料）。 它只是一串隨機編號，用來記住你的作品、分數與房間座位。

2. 你主動提供的內容

  • 暱稱：你自己取的顯示名稱，會出現在計分板與房間名單。請不要使用真實姓名或個資。
  • 畫作與猜題文字：你在遊戲中畫的筆畫、以及猜題輸入的文字。發布到「塗鴉牆／每日挑戰」的作品其他玩家看得到。
  • 你出的題目：在「你畫我猜」的大廳可以自己出一題。開始前其他人看不到題目內容（只看得到你已經出好了）； 那一回合開始後，題目會像一般題目一樣顯示給該猜的人，揭曉時也會公開「這題是誰出的」。
  • 表情反應：觀戰時按的表情會即時傳給正在畫的人，不會存下來。
  • 自訂大頭貼（選填）：你可以從相簿挑一張照片當頭像。我們只會取得你選的那一張——App 不會讀取你的整個相簿。

3. 遊戲與使用紀錄

  • 等級、經驗值、完成的每日挑戰、對局結果等遊戲進度。
  • 每日場次計數：免費玩家的「你畫我猜／畫畫PK」每天有場次上限，所以我們會記下 「你的匿名帳號編號、日期、模式、今天玩了幾場」。記在伺服器是為了讓重裝 App 不會直接把次數歸零。 只有這四個欄位，不含你畫了什麼。買了 Pro 就不受上限限制，也不會再送出這筆計數。
  • 基本的使用事件（例如「開啟了哪個模式」），用來了解哪些玩法受歡迎、把遊戲做得更好。

4. 我們不蒐集

  • 不要姓名、email、電話、地址、生日、性別。
  • 不蒐集精確位置。
  • 沒有廣告 SDK、沒有第三方分析 SDK、沒有 IDFA／廣告識別碼、不做跨 App 追蹤。
  • 不會把你的資料賣給任何人。

二、我們為什麼需要這些

  • 讓遊戲能玩：保存作品、計分、配對房間、接力輪次。
  • 社群功能：把作品顯示在塗鴉牆與每日挑戰上給其他玩家看。
  • 安全與審核：處理檢舉、移除不當內容、停用濫用帳號。
  • 改善遊戲：用彙總後的使用統計決定要加強哪些玩法。

我們不會用你的資料做廣告投放或個人化行銷。

三、附近連線與 iCloud

  • 附近連線（本地網路）：兩種情況會用到 Apple 的 MultipeerConnectivity，直接和同一個 Wi-Fi／藍牙範圍內的裝置連線： 鄰近模式整場都不經過我們的伺服器；線上房間則是在大家剛好在同一個地方時， 把「正在畫的即時筆畫」改走本地傳送（比較順、也比較省流量），其餘的遊戲資料照舊走伺服器。 兩種都只在你們的裝置之間傳遞。系統會詢問你的「本地網路」權限。
  • iCloud（選用）：開啟後，你的作品會備份到你自己的 iCloud 帳號，由 Apple 保管，我們無法存取。

四、AI 怎麼處理你的畫

為了幫你的畫評分、辨識「餵怪獸」與「畫畫 PK」裡你畫了什麼、比對 COPYCAT 畫得像不像、產生遊戲題目，以及產生每日挑戰的示範作品，本 App 會把 那張畫的圖檔與當天的題目文字傳送給 Google 的生成式 AI 服務（Gemini API）處理。

其中「畫畫 PK」比較特別：比賽進行中，你畫到一半的圖會持續送出去辨識 （放開筆之後約 0.4 秒送一次、最快每 2.5 秒一次），這樣才知道 AI 什麼時候認得出你畫的東西、並以此計時。 送的一樣只有圖與題目，畫完就不再送。

  • 傳過去的只有圖與題目。不會傳送你的暱稱、帳號編號或任何能辨識你的資料。
  • 用途只有評分、辨識與產生示範圖，不會用來投放廣告或個人化行銷。
  • 該服務對資料的處理另適用 Google 的隱私政策與其 API 條款。
  • 鄰近模式完全不使用 AI，資料不離開你們的裝置。

塗鴉牆上標著「AI 生成」的圖，是 AI 依當天題目畫的示範作品，不是任何玩家的作品。

五、購買（Drawverse Pro）

Drawverse Pro 是一次買斷的 App 內購買項目（非訂閱）。 付款完全由 Apple 處理——我們看不到、也不會保存你的信用卡、Apple ID 或任何付款資料。 我們只從 Apple 取得「這個 Apple ID 有沒有買過」的收據狀態，用來解鎖功能。退款請直接向 Apple 申請。

六、資料保存在哪裡

線上資料存放在 Supabase（我們的資料處理商）所託管的資料庫與儲存空間。 資料在傳輸過程中以 HTTPS/TLS 加密。我們只保留讓服務運作所需的時間；你刪除資料後即從資料庫移除。

另有一家次處理商：Google（Gemini API）， 僅用於第四章所述的評分、辨識與示範圖生成。除此之外沒有其他第三方會拿到你的資料。

七、你的權利：隨時刪除

在 App 裡打開 設定 → 刪除我的資料，即可永久刪除：
  • 這台裝置上的所有本機紀錄（作品、進度、封鎖名單、你自己畫的簽名）；
  • 雲端的個人檔案、每日作品、評分與反應、大頭貼照片；
  • 以及那個匿名帳號本身。
這個動作無法復原。你也可以寄信到下方信箱要求我們協助刪除。

八、兒童隱私

Drawverse 適合各年齡層，且不要求任何個人身分資料。我們不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。 由於遊戲包含其他玩家畫的內容，我們建議家長陪同年幼孩童遊玩，並善用 App 內的「檢舉」與「封鎖」功能。 若你認為孩童提供了個資給我們，請來信，我們會儘速刪除。

九、使用者內容與審核

塗鴉牆與每日挑戰上的作品是其他玩家畫的。我們對冒犯、騷擾或不當內容零容忍： 每張作品都可以「檢舉」，你也可以「封鎖」某位玩家——封鎖後你不會再看到他的任何作品。 我們會處理檢舉、移除違規內容並停用該帳號。

十、政策變更

若本政策有重大變更，我們會更新本頁上方的日期，並在 App 內適當提示。

十一、聯絡我們

任何隱私相關問題、或想要求存取／刪除資料，請寄信至 ctwu95@gmail.com，我們會在 7 個工作天內回覆。

Drawverse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Drawverse”

In one sentence: Drawverse requires no sign-up, asks for no name or email, shows no ads, performs no tracking, and sells no data. We store only what the game needs: an anonymous account ID, the nickname you choose, the drawings you make, and basic gameplay records. You can delete all of it at any time via Settings → Delete my data.

1. What we collect

Anonymous account ID

On first launch we create an anonymous account — no email, phone number, or identity information is required. It is a random identifier used to remember your artwork, score, and seat in a room.

Content you provide

  • Nickname — a display name you choose, shown on scoreboards and in rooms. Please do not use your real name.
  • Drawings and guesses — the strokes you draw and the text you type when guessing. Artwork published to the community wall or daily challenge is visible to other players.
  • Topics you write — in a guessing-game lobby you may submit one topic. Others cannot see its text beforehand (only that you have submitted one); once that round starts it is shown like any other topic, and the author is named at the reveal.
  • Reactions — emoji you tap while spectating are relayed live to the player drawing and are not stored.
  • Custom avatar (optional) — you may pick one photo from your library. We receive only the photo you select; the app never gains access to your whole photo library.

Gameplay records

  • Level, XP, completed daily challenges and match results.
  • A daily match counter. Free players have a daily limit on guessing games and Drawing PK, so we record your anonymous account id, the date, the mode, and how many matches you have played that day. Keeping it on the server is what stops a reinstall from resetting the count. Those four fields are all it holds — nothing about what you drew. With Pro there is no limit and no counter is sent.
  • Basic in-house usage events (e.g. which mode was opened) to understand which modes players enjoy.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone, address, birthday or gender.
  • No precise location.
  • No advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs, no IDFA, no cross-app tracking.
  • We never sell your data.

2. Why we need it

  • To run the game — saving artwork, scoring, matchmaking, relay turn order.
  • Community features — showing artwork to other players.
  • Safety and moderation — handling reports, removing objectionable content, banning abusive accounts.
  • Improving the game — aggregate usage statistics.

We do not use your data for advertising or personalised marketing.

3. Nearby play and iCloud

  • Local network — Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity is used in two cases. Nearby play never touches our servers at all. Online rooms send live in-progress strokes peer-to-peer when everyone happens to be in the same place (lower latency, less data); everything else still goes through the server. In both cases that traffic stays between your devices. iOS will ask for Local Network permission.
  • iCloud (optional) — when enabled, your artwork is backed up to your own iCloud account, held by Apple. We cannot access it.

4. How AI processes your drawings

To score your drawings, to recognise what you drew in “Feed the Monster” and “Drawing PK”, to judge how closely your COPYCAT attempt matches, to generate game prompts, and to produce the daily example artwork, the app sends the image of that drawing and the prompt word for the day to Google’s generative AI service (the Gemini API).

Drawing PK works differently in one respect: while a match is running your in-progress drawing is sent repeatedly — roughly 0.4 seconds after you lift the pen, and at most once every 2.5 seconds — because the game is timed by how soon the AI can recognise what you are drawing. It still sends only the image and the prompt, and stops when the round ends.

  • Only the image and the prompt are sent. Your nickname, account ID and anything else that could identify you are not.
  • It is used only for scoring, recognition and generating example art — never for advertising or profiling.
  • That service’s handling of the data is additionally governed by Google’s privacy policy and API terms.
  • Nearby play uses no AI at all; that data never leaves your devices.

Images labelled “AI 生成” on the community wall are example pieces drawn by AI from the day’s prompt. They are not any player’s work.

5. Purchases (Drawverse Pro)

Drawverse Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store your card details, Apple ID or any payment information. We receive only the receipt status of whether this Apple ID has purchased, in order to unlock the features. Refunds are requested from Apple.

6. Where data is stored

Online data is hosted on Supabase, our data processor. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We keep it only as long as needed to operate the service.

There is one further sub-processor: Google (Gemini API), used only for the scoring, recognition and example-art generation described in section 4. No other third party receives your data.

7. Your rights: delete at any time

Open Settings → Delete my data in the app to permanently delete all local records, your cloud profile, submitted artwork, ratings and reactions, avatar photo, and the anonymous account itself. This cannot be undone. You may also email us for assistance.

8. Children’s privacy

Drawverse is suitable for all ages and requires no personally identifying information. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the game shows content drawn by other players, we recommend parental guidance for younger children and encourage use of the in-app Report and Block tools.

9. User content and moderation

We have zero tolerance for objectionable or abusive content. Every piece of artwork can be reported, and you can block any player — once blocked you will never see their content again. We act on reports, remove violating content and disable offending accounts.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For any privacy question, or to request access or deletion, email ctwu95@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 business days.
