Drawverse 隱私政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 17 日 · 適用於 iOS App「Drawverse（畫到崩潰）」

一句話版本：Drawverse 不需要你註冊、不要你的姓名或 email、沒有廣告、不做追蹤、不賣資料。 我們只保存讓遊戲能運作的東西：一個匿名帳號編號、你取的暱稱、你畫的圖，以及基本的遊戲紀錄。 你隨時可以在 App 裡「設定 → 刪除我的資料」把這些全部刪掉。

一、我們蒐集什麼

1. 匿名帳號編號

你第一次開啟 App 時，系統會自動建立一個匿名帳號（不需要 email、電話或任何個人身分資料）。 它只是一串隨機編號，用來記住你的作品、分數與房間座位。

2. 你主動提供的內容

  • 暱稱：你自己取的顯示名稱，會出現在計分板與房間名單。請不要使用真實姓名或個資。
  • 畫作與猜題文字：你在遊戲中畫的筆畫、以及猜題輸入的文字。發布到「塗鴉牆」的作品其他玩家看得到。 在「你畫我猜」與「畫畫PK」的對局進行中，你畫到一半的畫面也會即時傳給同房的人看 （「畫畫PK」是縮圖，且進度超前你的人會被打馬賽克）；對局結束後這些即時畫面就不再保留。
    畫畫PK 的結算畫廊：一場打完之後，同房的人看得到彼此「通過」的那幾張畫 （沒通過與跳過的不顯示）。這些筆畫本來就存在伺服器上供結算使用，畫廊只是把同一個房間內的人 開放互看；不會發布到塗鴉牆，也沒有對外連結。畫廊上的每一張都可以檢舉，也可以封鎖對方。
  • 表情反應：觀戰時按的表情會即時傳給正在畫的人，不會存下來。
  • 自訂大頭貼（選填）：你可以從相簿挑一張照片當頭像。我們只會取得你選的那一張——App 不會讀取你的整個相簿。

3. 遊戲與使用紀錄

  • 等級、經驗值、對局結果等遊戲進度。
  • 每日場次計數：免費玩家的「你畫我猜／畫畫PK」每天有場次上限，所以我們會記下 「你的匿名帳號編號、日期、模式、今天玩了幾場」。記在伺服器是為了讓重裝 App 不會直接把次數歸零。 只有這四個欄位，不含你畫了什麼。訂閱 Drawverse Plus 就不受上限限制，也不會再送出這筆計數。
  • 網路區段的單向雜湊：房間列表上有一個「同網路」的小標記，讓你看得出哪幾間房 和你在同一個網路。為了做到這件事，開房時我們會把當下連線的網路區段 （IPv4 的前三段／IPv6 的前三組）加鹽後做成一段不可反推的雜湊存起來。 我們不保存你的 IP 位址，存的只是那段雜湊，而且它只拿來和別間房的雜湊比對相不相同。 這和位置無關——我們仍然不蒐集任何位置資訊。
  • 基本的使用事件（例如「開啟了哪個模式」），用來了解哪些玩法受歡迎、把遊戲做得更好。

4. 我們不蒐集

  • 不要姓名、email、電話、地址、生日、性別。
  • 不蒐集精確位置。
  • 沒有廣告 SDK、沒有第三方分析 SDK、沒有 IDFA／廣告識別碼、不做跨 App 追蹤。
  • 不會把你的資料賣給任何人。

二、我們為什麼需要這些

  • 讓遊戲能玩：保存作品、計分、配對房間、接力輪次。
  • 社群功能：把作品顯示在塗鴉牆上給其他玩家看。
  • 安全與審核：處理檢舉、移除不當內容、停用濫用帳號。
  • 改善遊戲：用彙總後的使用統計決定要加強哪些玩法。

我們不會用你的資料做廣告投放或個人化行銷。

三、附近連線與 iCloud

  • 找附近正在等人的房間：在房間列表那一頁，App 會用 Apple 的 MultipeerConnectivity 在本地網路上尋找附近同樣在等人的 Drawverse 房間。 廣播出去的只有房號（就是印在大廳、你會唸給朋友聽的那六個字）， 不含你的暱稱、帳號編號或任何畫作。這一段只做尋找、不會建立連線， 找到的結果也不會送到我們的伺服器；離開房間列表就停止。
    房間列表只會顯示附近的房間，所以沒有允許「本地網路」權限的話，列表會是空的。 這不影響其他玩法，而且用房號一樣進得去——房號不受「附近」限制， 朋友把房號傳給你就能加入。
  • 附近連線（本地網路）：另外兩種情況會用到 MultipeerConnectivity，直接和同一個 Wi-Fi／藍牙範圍內的裝置連線： 鄰近模式整場都不經過我們的伺服器；線上房間則是在大家剛好在同一個地方時， 把「正在畫的即時筆畫」改走本地傳送（比較順、也比較省流量），其餘的遊戲資料照舊走伺服器。 兩種都只在你們的裝置之間傳遞。系統會詢問你的「本地網路」權限。
  • iCloud（選用）：開啟後，你的作品會備份到你自己的 iCloud 帳號，由 Apple 保管，我們無法存取。

四、AI 怎麼處理你的畫

為了辨識「餵怪獸」與「畫畫 PK」裡你畫了什麼，本 App 會把 那張畫的圖檔與那一題的題目文字傳送給 Google 的生成式 AI 服務（Gemini API）處理。

另外，遊戲的題庫也是由同一個服務每天產生的（會搭配 Google 搜尋抓當天的熱門話題）。 這一段完全不涉及你的資料——純粹是伺服器在產生題目，沒有送出任何玩家的畫或文字。

其中「畫畫 PK」比較特別：比賽進行中，你畫到一半的圖會持續送出去辨識 （放開筆之後約 0.35 秒送一次，連續畫不停時至少每 1.5 秒一次，兩次之間最短間隔 1 秒）， 這樣才知道 AI 什麼時候認得出你畫的東西、並以此決定何時換下一題。 送的一樣只有圖與題目，那一題結束就不再送。 AI 的判讀結果（「AI 看成：○○」）只會回給你自己的畫面，不會傳給同房的其他人。

  • 傳過去的只有圖與題目。不會傳送你的暱稱、帳號編號或任何能辨識你的資料。
  • 用途只有辨識與出題，不會用來投放廣告或個人化行銷。
  • 該服務對資料的處理另適用 Google 的隱私政策與其 API 條款。
  • 鄰近模式完全不使用 AI，資料不離開你們的裝置。

五、訂閱（Drawverse Plus）

Drawverse Plus 是每月自動續訂的 App 內訂閱（每月 NT$90）。 付款完全由 Apple 處理——我們看不到、也不會保存你的信用卡、Apple ID 或任何付款資料。 我們只從 Apple 取得「這個 Apple ID 目前有沒有在訂閱」的收據狀態，用來解鎖功能； 這個判斷發生在你的裝置上（StoreKit），我們的伺服器不會保存你的訂閱狀態。 取消訂閱請到「設定 → 你的 Apple ID → 訂閱項目」，退款請直接向 Apple 申請。

六、資料保存在哪裡

線上資料存放在 Supabase（我們的資料處理商）所託管的資料庫與儲存空間。 資料在傳輸過程中以 HTTPS/TLS 加密。我們只保留讓服務運作所需的時間；你刪除資料後即從資料庫移除。

另有一家次處理商：Google（Gemini API）， 僅用於第四章所述的辨識與題目產生。除此之外沒有其他第三方會拿到你的資料。

七、你的權利：隨時刪除

在 App 裡打開 設定 → 刪除我的資料，即可永久刪除：
  • 這台裝置上的所有本機紀錄（作品、進度、封鎖名單、你自己畫的簽名）；
  • 雲端的個人檔案、發布的作品、反應與大頭貼照片；
  • 以及那個匿名帳號本身。
這個動作無法復原。你也可以寄信到下方信箱要求我們協助刪除。

八、兒童隱私

Drawverse 適合各年齡層，且不要求任何個人身分資料。我們不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。 由於遊戲包含其他玩家畫的內容，我們建議家長陪同年幼孩童遊玩，並善用 App 內的「檢舉」與「封鎖」功能。 若你認為孩童提供了個資給我們，請來信，我們會儘速刪除。

九、使用者內容與審核

塗鴉牆上的作品，以及畫畫PK 結算畫廊、你畫我猜揭曉頁裡的畫，都是其他玩家畫的。我們對冒犯、騷擾或不當內容零容忍：

  • 路人的塗鴉牆：打開任何一個塗鴉牆，右上角的「⋯」→「檢舉塗鴉牆」或「封鎖這個人」。
  • 畫畫PK 結算畫廊、你畫我猜揭曉頁：每一張別人的縮圖右上角都有一顆小旗子， 按下去是「檢舉這個玩家」或「封鎖這個人」。
  • 封鎖之後，那個人的作品不會再出現在任何畫面上；要解除到「設定 → 封鎖名單」。
  • 同一個對象被多位不同的使用者檢舉時，系統會先自動把它下架，再由我們人工審視。 那只是「先收起來等人看」；滿 24 小時我們還沒看完的，系統會自動把它重新公開。

我們會在 24 小時內處理檢舉、移除違規內容並停用該帳號。

如果被下架的是你的塗鴉牆：App 裡會直接告訴你目前的狀態（正在覆核、或是已判定違規）， 不會讓你自己猜。經人工判定違規的，你可以修改內容後申請重新覆核—— 為了處理這件事，我們會記下這次下架申訴過幾次、以及最後一次申訴的時間。 就這兩個欄位，不含申訴的內容；你刪除資料時它們跟著整面牆一起消失。

十、政策變更

若本政策有重大變更，我們會更新本頁上方的日期，並在 App 內適當提示。

十一、聯絡我們

任何隱私相關問題、或想要求存取／刪除資料，請寄信至 ctwu95@gmail.com，我們會在 7 個工作天內回覆。

Drawverse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Drawverse”

In one sentence: Drawverse requires no sign-up, asks for no name or email, shows no ads, performs no tracking, and sells no data. We store only what the game needs: an anonymous account ID, the nickname you choose, the drawings you make, and basic gameplay records. You can delete all of it at any time via Settings → Delete my data.

1. What we collect

Anonymous account ID

On first launch we create an anonymous account — no email, phone number, or identity information is required. It is a random identifier used to remember your artwork, score, and seat in a room.

Content you provide

  • Nickname — a display name you choose, shown on scoreboards and in rooms. Please do not use your real name.
  • Drawings and guesses — the strokes you draw and the text you type when guessing. Artwork published to the community wall is visible to other players. During a guessing game or a Drawing PK match your in-progress drawing is also relayed live to the other players in the room (as a thumbnail in Drawing PK, blurred for anyone ahead of you). Those live frames are not kept once the match ends.
    The Drawing PK results gallery: once a match ends, everyone in that room can see the drawings each player cleared (failed and skipped prompts are not shown). Those strokes were already stored on the server for scoring; the gallery simply lets the people in that one room see each other’s. Nothing is published to the community wall and there is no public link. Every drawing there can be reported, and its author blocked.
  • Reactions — emoji you tap while spectating are relayed live to the player drawing and are not stored.
  • Custom avatar (optional) — you may pick one photo from your library. We receive only the photo you select; the app never gains access to your whole photo library.

Gameplay records

  • Level, XP and match results.
  • A daily match counter. Free players have a daily limit on guessing games and Drawing PK, so we record your anonymous account id, the date, the mode, and how many matches you have played that day. Keeping it on the server is what stops a reinstall from resetting the count. Those four fields are all it holds — nothing about what you drew. With Drawverse Plus there is no limit and no counter is sent.
  • A one-way hash of your network segment. The room list shows a small “same network” marker so you can tell which rooms are on the same network as you. To do that, when a room is created we store a salted, non-reversible hash of the network segment in use at that moment (the first three parts of an IPv4 address, or the first three groups of an IPv6 one). We do not store your IP address — only that hash, and only to compare it with other rooms’ hashes. This is not location data; we still collect no location at all.
  • Basic in-house usage events (e.g. which mode was opened) to understand which modes players enjoy.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone, address, birthday or gender.
  • No precise location.
  • No advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs, no IDFA, no cross-app tracking.
  • We never sell your data.

2. Why we need it

  • To run the game — saving artwork, scoring, matchmaking, relay turn order.
  • Community features — showing artwork to other players.
  • Safety and moderation — handling reports, removing objectionable content, banning abusive accounts.
  • Improving the game — aggregate usage statistics.

We do not use your data for advertising or personalised marketing.

3. Nearby play and iCloud

  • Finding nearby rooms — on the room-list screen the app uses Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity to look for nearby Drawverse rooms that are also waiting for players. All that is broadcast is the room code — the same six characters shown in the lobby that you would read out to a friend. No nickname, account ID or artwork. This step only looks: it opens no connection, and what it finds is never sent to our servers. It stops when you leave the room list.
    Room lists only show nearby rooms, so if you decline Local Network permission the list will be empty. This does not affect any other mode, and joining with a room code still works — room codes are not restricted to nearby players, so a friend can simply send you one.
  • Local network — MultipeerConnectivity is also used in two other cases. Nearby play never touches our servers at all. Online rooms send live in-progress strokes peer-to-peer when everyone happens to be in the same place (lower latency, less data); everything else still goes through the server. In both cases that traffic stays between your devices. iOS will ask for Local Network permission.
  • iCloud (optional) — when enabled, your artwork is backed up to your own iCloud account, held by Apple. We cannot access it.

4. How AI processes your drawings

To recognise what you drew in “Feed the Monster” and “Drawing PK”, the app sends the image of that drawing and the prompt word for that round to Google’s generative AI service (the Gemini API).

The game’s prompt bank is also generated daily by that same service (using Google Search to pick up current topics). That step involves none of your data — it is our server generating prompts, with no player drawing or text sent.

Drawing PK works differently in one respect: while a match is running your in-progress drawing is sent repeatedly — roughly 0.35 seconds after you lift the pen, at least every 1.5 seconds if you keep drawing without pausing, and never more often than once per second — because the game advances as soon as the AI recognises what you are drawing. It still sends only the image and the prompt, and stops when that prompt is cleared. What the AI thinks it sees (“AI 看成: …”) is shown only on your own screen and is not sent to the other players in the room.

  • Only the image and the prompt are sent. Your nickname, account ID and anything else that could identify you are not.
  • It is used only for recognition and prompt generation — never for advertising or profiling.
  • That service’s handling of the data is additionally governed by Google’s privacy policy and API terms.
  • Nearby play uses no AI at all; that data never leaves your devices.

5. Subscription (Drawverse Plus)

Drawverse Plus is an auto-renewing monthly in-app subscription (NT$90 per month). Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store your card details, Apple ID or any payment information. We receive only the receipt status of whether this Apple ID currently has an active subscription, in order to unlock the features; that check happens on your device via StoreKit, and our servers never store your subscription status. Cancel under Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. Refunds are requested from Apple.

6. Where data is stored

Online data is hosted on Supabase, our data processor. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We keep it only as long as needed to operate the service.

There is one further sub-processor: Google (Gemini API), used only for the recognition and prompt generation described in section 4. No other third party receives your data.

7. Your rights: delete at any time

Open Settings → Delete my data in the app to permanently delete all local records, your cloud profile, submitted artwork, ratings and reactions, avatar photo, and the anonymous account itself. This cannot be undone. You may also email us for assistance.

8. Children’s privacy

Drawverse is suitable for all ages and requires no personally identifying information. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the game shows content drawn by other players, we recommend parental guidance for younger children and encourage use of the in-app Report and Block tools.

9. User content and moderation

We have zero tolerance for objectionable or abusive content, whether on the community wall or in a Drawing PK results gallery.

  • Community wall — open any wall and use the “⋯” menu at the top right: Report, or Block this person.
  • Drawing PK results gallery — every thumbnail by another player carries a small flag button for the same two actions.
  • Once blocked, that person’s content never appears anywhere again; undo it under Settings → Blocked list.
  • Content reported by several different users is hidden automatically before we review it by hand. That is only setting it aside pending review — if we have not finished looking 24 hours later, it is republished automatically.

We act on reports within 24 hours, remove violating content and disable offending accounts.

If the wall taken down is yours: the app tells you which state you are in — under review, or judged to be in violation — rather than leaving you to guess. For a human decision you may edit and request another review, and to administer that we record how many appeals this takedown has used and when the last one was sent. Those two fields only, never the content of an appeal, and they are deleted along with the wall when you delete your data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For any privacy question, or to request access or deletion, email ctwu95@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 business days.
