常見問題

我看到不舒服的作品，怎麼辦？

  • 路人的塗鴉牆：打開任何一個塗鴉牆，右上角的「⋯」→「這張有問題」或「封鎖這個人」。
  • 畫畫PK 的結算畫廊：別人的每一張縮圖右上角都有一顆小旗子，按下去就是檢舉或封鎖。

我們會在 24 小時內處理，移除違規內容並停用帳號。同一個對象被多人檢舉時會先自動下架，等我們看過再決定。 封鎖之後，那個人的作品不會再出現在任何畫面上；要解除到「設定 → 封鎖名單」。

按下去之後按鈕會顯示「已收到檢舉」——那代表伺服器真的收到了。如果顯示「沒送出去」， 請再按一次，或直接寄信到 ctwu95@gmail.com，24 小時的承諾一樣算數。

「畫畫PK」怎麼玩？

有兩個入口。快速開畫會把你和其他正在排隊的線上玩家自動湊成一場：目標 4 人， 人滿就立刻開始；等了 20 秒還不滿 4 人，只要已經有 2 個人就會開始。 不過剛進來的人一定看得到大廳至少 8 秒——不會才剛加入畫面就直接開打； 只有在那間房已經等滿 1 分鐘的時候，才不再為任何人延後。 只有你一個人不會開——一個人的佇列會一直等下去，直到第 2 個人進來。 沒有房主，所以不必等誰按開始，也沒有房號。還在排隊的時候可以隨時取消， 取消不會扣場次（場次是真的開打才算）。 人數還不夠的時候畫面會直接寫「還差幾個人」，排不到也會老實說，並建議你改走另一條路。

揪附近的玩就是自己開一間房、或加入別人開的房。 房間列表只會顯示附近玩家開的房（同一個 Wi-Fi 或藍牙範圍內）； 但房號不受這個限制——朋友把房號傳給你，人在哪裡都進得來。

一場開始之後，同一個房間的人拿到同一份題目、同樣順序，各畫各的。AI 認出你畫的東西就自動換下一題， 全場 3 分鐘，通過最多題的人贏。不會畫可以按住「PASS」換一題，一場最多 5 次—— 跳掉的題目不會再回來。畫到一半的小畫面其他人看得到，但進度超前你的人會打馬賽克，免得你先看到還沒輪到的題目。

AI 看不懂的時候會告訴你它看成什麼——題目上方會出現「AI 看成：○○」，照著它差的地方補就好。

有固定顏色的東西要上色。番茄汁本來就是紅的，畫成黃的 AI 不會算你過； 這時候會顯示「顏色不對喔」。本來就沒有固定顏色的東西（杯子、車子、書）用線稿畫就可以，不必上色。

打完之後，同一個房間的人看得到彼此通過的那幾張（沒通過和跳過的不顯示）。 只有那個房間裡看得到，不會發布到塗鴉牆。

每天可以玩幾場？

沒有訂閱的話，「你畫我猜」每天 5 場、「畫畫PK」每天 3 場 （快速開畫和揪附近的玩算同一個額度；排隊中途取消不扣，真的開打才算一場）。 其餘玩法（隨性的畫、塗鴉牆、鄰近模式）不限次數。 場次以你所在時區的當地日期每天重置，而且記在伺服器上——重裝 App 或改系統時間都不會歸零。 訂閱 Drawverse Plus 就沒有這個上限。

Drawverse Plus 是什麼？訂了會有什麼？

每月 NT$90 的自動續訂訂閱。解鎖彩虹筆、 自訂顏色、開惡搞房的能力，以及取消每日場次上限。 加入別人開的惡搞房不需要訂閱；刷子、毛筆、粉筆、水彩筆自 2026 年 9 月起已免費開放，不用訂閱也能用。

換手機了，要再買一次嗎？

不用。訂閱綁在你的 Apple ID 上，用同一個 Apple ID 登入，到訂閱頁按一下「回復購買」就會回來。

怎麼取消訂閱？

到 iPhone 的「設定 → 你的 Apple ID → 訂閱項目 → Drawverse Plus」按取消， 或在 App 的訂閱頁按「管理訂閱」。刪除 App 並不會取消訂閱。 取消後到當期結束前都還能用，之後彩虹筆與自訂顏色會回到鎖定狀態、每日場次上限也會恢復—— 但你先前畫的作品不會消失，也不會被遮蔽。

想退費怎麼辦？

App 內購買項目的退款一律由 Apple 處理，我們無法代為退款。請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 提出申請。

畫到一半那些表情是什麼？

是在旁邊看的人按的即時反應，只會飄過去、不會存下來。輪到你畫的時候才看得到。

簽名功能怎麼用？

在「隨性的畫」畫完按完成時，會問你要不要簽名。 簽名可以從內建字體挑一種（花體、縮寫、手寫、圓體、毛筆、記號），也可以自己手寫一個—— 自己畫的簽名可以選筆和顏色。選好之後拖到畫面上任何畫得到的位置，還能縮放和旋轉。

簽名只存在你自己的手機裡，不會上傳（除非你把它蓋在作品上一起發布）。 要改或刪掉，到「設定 → 我的簽名」。不想每次被問，把「畫完問我要不要簽名」關掉就好。

評語和分數是誰給的？

是 AI 看的。餵怪獸與畫畫PK 的辨識會把你的畫送到第三方 AI 服務處理， 詳見隱私權政策第四節。遊戲題目也是 AI 每天產生的，但那一段不涉及你的資料。 AI 會看走眼也會亂講，判定純屬娛樂，不要太認真。

畫布上的筆拿反了？

設定裡有兩個分開的選項：

  • 「畫畫時」選拿筆或用手。選「用手」就不會在畫布上畫那支筆。
  • 「慣用手」選左撇子或右撇子，決定筆的朝向。 選了「用手」之後這一項會變灰——沒有筆就沒有方向可以調。

怎麼解除封鎖？

到「設定 → 封鎖名單」，找到那個人按「解除」。

怎麼刪除我的資料／帳號？

「設定 → 刪除我的資料」。這會刪掉本機所有紀錄，以及雲端的個人檔案、作品、大頭貼與匿名帳號本身。 這個動作無法復原。若你在 App 內操作有困難，寄信給我們也可以代為處理。

需要註冊帳號嗎？

不需要。Drawverse 不要 email、不要密碼、不要姓名。第一次開啟時會自動建立一個匿名編號，就這樣。

我換手機了，作品會跟著過去嗎？

打開「設定 → iCloud 同步」，作品會備份到你自己的 iCloud，在同一個 Apple ID 的裝置上可以取回。

「鄰近模式」連不上？

鄰近模式用本地網路連線，請確認：兩台裝置在同一個 Wi-Fi、藍牙已開啟， 並且在「iOS 設定 → Drawverse → 本地網路」允許權限。

房間列表是空的 / 看不到附近的房間？

房間列表只會顯示附近玩家開的房，所以旁邊沒有人在開房的時候它本來就是空的。 如果旁邊明明有人開了卻看不到，請確認：兩台裝置在同一個 Wi-Fi、藍牙已開啟， 並且在「iOS 設定 → Drawverse → 本地網路」允許權限——沒有這個權限，列表永遠會是空的。

不管列表看不看得到，用房號都進得去：請對方把大廳上的六位房號唸給你， 在「有房號也行」輸入就可以，人不在附近也沒關係。 線上找不到人的時候，畫畫PK 還可以用「快速開畫」和其他線上玩家配對。

斷線了、或 App 被關掉，那一場還回得去嗎？

回得去。畫畫PK 比賽中閃退的話，重開 App 之後入口頁會出現「那場還在打」，點一下就接回原本的進度 （分數、題目、剩下的時間都還在）；那一場如果已經結束了，便條就不會出現。 比賽中網路斷掉時，題目上方會出現紅色的「連線斷了，重連中…」——看到這一句就是畫的東西沒有送出去， 不是你畫得不夠像，網路一回來就會自動接上。 你畫我猜同樣有「剛剛的遊戲還在」的入口。

線上房間進不去 / 一直轉圈？

多半是網路問題，請先切換 Wi-Fi／行動網路再試一次。若持續發生，請寄信告訴我們發生的時間與房號。

可以離線玩嗎？

可以。「隨性的畫」不需要網路，「你畫我猜」的鄰近模式也只用本地連線；線上房間與畫畫PK 需要連線。

餵怪獸的「加時間」是怎麼算的？

餵怪獸是「你畫我猜」裡的候場小關卡——還沒輪到你畫、也還不能觀戰的時候會出現。 怪獸認出你畫的東西並吃掉時，下一回合就會多給你幾秒鐘，畫面上的沙漏會顯示目前累積到多少秒。 牠沒認出來就不加時間——按「不會畫，跳過」換一題不會被扣分。

FAQ

I saw content that upset me. What do I do?

  • Community wall — open any wall and use the “⋯” menu at the top right: Report, or Block this person.
  • Drawing PK results gallery — every thumbnail by another player carries a small flag button for the same two actions.

We review reports within 24 hours, remove violating content and disable the account. Anything reported by several different people is hidden automatically until we have looked at it. Once blocked, that person’s content never appears again; undo it under Settings → Blocked list.

The button turns into “Report received” only once the server has actually stored it. If it says the report could not be sent, tap again or email ctwu95@gmail.com — the same 24-hour commitment applies.

How does Drawing PK work?

There are two ways in. Quick Match pairs you automatically with other players who are queueing: the target is four players and the match starts as soon as it is full; if 20 seconds pass with fewer, it starts anyway provided at least two people are there. Whoever joined most recently always gets at least 8 seconds in the lobby, so a match never begins the instant you arrive; only once a queue has been open for a full minute does it stop waiting for anyone. On your own it will not start — the queue simply keeps waiting until a second person joins. There is no host, so nobody has to press start, and there is no room code. You can cancel at any time while queueing, and cancelling does not use up a match — only a match that actually starts counts. While there are not enough players the screen says how many more are needed, and if nobody else is queueing it says so plainly and points you to the other route.

Play with people nearby means opening your own room or joining someone else’s. The room list only shows rooms opened by players near you (same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth range), but room codes are not restricted that way — if a friend sends you a code, you can join from anywhere.

Once a match starts, everyone in the room gets the same prompts in the same order and draws independently. As soon as the AI recognises what you drew, you move to the next prompt. A match lasts 3 minutes, and whoever clears the most prompts wins. If you are stuck, hold PASS to swap a prompt — five times per match, and a skipped prompt never comes back. Other players can see your drawing in progress, but anyone ahead of you is blurred so you do not see prompts you have not reached yet.

When the AI cannot tell what it is, it tells you what it sees — “AI 看成: …” appears above the prompt, so you know what to fix.

Objects with a fixed colour have to be coloured in. Tomato juice is red; drawn in yellow it will not pass, and you will see “顏色不對喔”. Objects with no fixed colour (a cup, a car, a book) are fine as a plain line drawing.

After the match, everyone in the room can see the drawings each player cleared (failed and skipped prompts are not shown). It is visible only inside that room and is never published to the community wall.

How many matches can I play per day?

Without a subscription: 5 guessing games and 3 Drawing PK matches per day (Quick Match and nearby rooms share the same allowance; cancelling while queueing does not count — only a match that actually starts does). Everything else — free drawing, the community wall and nearby play — is unlimited. The count resets daily on your local date and is stored on the server, so reinstalling the app or changing your system clock will not reset it. Drawverse Plus removes the limit.

What is Drawverse Plus?

An auto-renewing subscription at NT$90 per month. It unlocks the rainbow pen, custom colours, the ability to host prank-mode rooms, and removes the daily match limit on the guessing game and Drawing PK. Joining someone else's prank room does not require a subscription, and the brush, ink brush, chalk and watercolour pens are free for everyone.

I got a new phone. Do I have to buy it again?

No. The subscription is tied to your Apple ID. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore purchases on the subscription page.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Drawverse Plus on your iPhone and tap Cancel, or tap “Manage subscription” on the subscription page in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. You keep access until the end of the current period; after that the rainbow pen and custom colours lock again and the daily match limit returns — but drawings you already made are never removed or hidden.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple; we cannot issue them. Please file a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What are the faces that appear while I am drawing?

Live reactions tapped by the people watching. They float past and are not stored anywhere.

How does the signature feature work?

When you finish a drawing in free drawing, Drawverse asks whether you want to sign it. Pick one of the built-in styles (script, initials, handwriting, rounded, brush, mark) or draw your own — a hand-drawn signature lets you choose the pen and the colour. Then drag it anywhere on the canvas you could draw, and pinch or rotate to size it.

Your signature is stored only on your device and is never uploaded, unless you stamp it onto a drawing you publish. Change or delete it under Settings → My signature, and turn off “ask me when I finish” if you do not want the prompt.

Who writes the comments and scores?

An AI does. Feed-the-monster and Drawing PK recognition send your drawing to a third-party AI service — see section 4 of the Privacy Policy. Prompts are generated daily by the same service, but that step involves none of your data. The AI misreads drawings and makes things up, so treat its verdicts as entertainment.

The pen on the canvas points the wrong way

There are two separate settings:

  • “畫畫時” (When drawing) — 拿筆 (with a pen) or 用手 (with a finger). Choosing 用手 removes the pen from the canvas entirely.
  • “慣用手” (Handedness) — 左撇子 (left) or 右撇子 (right), which sets the direction the pen points. It greys out once you choose 用手: with no pen there is no direction to set.

How do I unblock someone?

Go to Settings → Blocked list and tap Unblock.

How do I delete my data / account?

Settings → Delete my data. This removes all local records plus your cloud profile, artwork, avatar and the anonymous account itself. It cannot be undone. Email us if you need help.

Do I need an account?

No. Drawverse asks for no email, password or name. An anonymous ID is created automatically on first launch.

I got a new phone — do my drawings follow me?

Turn on Settings → iCloud sync. Artwork is backed up to your own iCloud and restored on devices sharing your Apple ID.

Nearby play won’t connect

Nearby play uses the local network. Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, Bluetooth is on, and Local Network permission is allowed in iOS Settings → Drawverse.

The room list is empty / I cannot see nearby rooms

The room list only shows rooms opened by players near you, so when nobody nearby has a room open it is empty by design. If someone next to you has opened one and you still cannot see it, check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, that Bluetooth is on, and that Local Network permission is allowed in iOS Settings → Drawverse — without that permission the list will always be empty.

Either way, a room code always works: ask them to read out the six characters shown in their lobby and enter it under “有房號也行”. You do not have to be nearby. When nobody is around, Drawing PK also has Quick Match.

I got disconnected, or the app was closed — can I get back into the match?

Yes. If Drawing PK crashes mid-match, reopen the app and the entry screen shows “那場還在打” — tap it and you are back where you were, with your score, prompt and remaining time intact. If that match has already finished, the note does not appear. While you are offline during a match, a red “連線斷了，重連中…” appears above the prompt — that means your drawing is not reaching the server, not that it is not good enough; it reconnects by itself. The guessing game has the same “剛剛的遊戲還在” entry.

Can I play offline?

Yes — free drawing works offline, and nearby guessing games run entirely over the local network. Online rooms and Drawing PK need a connection.

How does the extra time in feed-the-monster work?

Feed-the-monster is the waiting mini-game inside the guessing game — it appears while it is not yet your turn to draw and there is nothing to spectate. Every time the monster recognises and eats what you drew, you get a few more seconds in the next round; the hourglass on screen shows how much you have banked. If it does not recognise the drawing you get nothing extra — and tapping skip for a new prompt costs you nothing.
