常見問題

我看到不舒服的作品，怎麼辦？

點開那張作品 → 按「這張有問題」檢舉。我們會在 24 小時內處理，移除違規內容並停用帳號。 同一件作品被多人檢舉時會先自動下架，等我們看過再決定。 你也可以按「封鎖這個人」，之後就不會再看到他的任何作品。

按下去之後按鈕會顯示「已收到檢舉」——那代表伺服器真的收到了。如果顯示「沒送出去」， 請再按一次，或直接寄信到 ctwu95@gmail.com，24 小時的承諾一樣算數。

Drawverse Pro 是什麼？買了會有什麼？

一次買斷的 App 內購買項目（非訂閱），解鎖刷子、毛筆、粉筆、水彩筆、彩虹筆五支筆、 自訂顏色，以及開惡搞房的能力。加入別人開的惡搞房不需要購買。

換手機了，要再買一次嗎？

不用。用同一個 Apple ID 登入，到「設定 → Drawverse Pro → 恢復購買」按一下就會回來。

想退費怎麼辦？

App 內購買項目的退款一律由 Apple 處理，我們無法代為退款。請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 提出申請。

可以自己出題嗎？

可以。在「你畫我猜」的大廳按出題，你出的那一題會用在你當第一棒的那一回合。 開始前別人只看得到「你已經出好了」，看不到內容。沒出題的人，那一回合就抽 AI 題。

揭曉時會公開這題是誰出的——因為猜對時「前一棒」拿的分最多，而那一棒就是出題的人， 公開作者純粹是讓大家看得到誰在出「貓」這種送分題。

畫到一半那些表情是什麼？

是在旁邊看的人按的即時反應，只會飄過去、不會存下來。輪到你畫的時候才看得到。

簽名功能怎麼用？

在「隨性的畫」和「每日挑戰」畫完按完成時，會問你要不要簽名。 簽名可以從內建字體挑一種（花體、縮寫、手寫、圓體、毛筆、記號），也可以自己手寫一個—— 自己畫的簽名可以選筆和顏色。選好之後拖到畫面上任何畫得到的位置，還能縮放和旋轉。

簽名只存在你自己的手機裡，不會上傳（除非你把它蓋在作品上一起發布）。 要改或刪掉，到「設定 → 我的簽名」。不想每次被問，把「畫完問我要不要簽名」關掉就好。

評語和分數是誰給的？

是 AI 給的。每日挑戰的評語與分數、餵怪獸和 COPYCAT 的辨識與比對、遊戲題目的產生、以及「今天大家的畫」裡的 AI 示範圖， 都會把你的畫送到第三方 AI 服務處理，詳見隱私權政策第四節。 AI 會看走眼也會亂講，分數純屬娛樂，不要太認真。

畫布上的筆拿反了？

到「設定 → 畫筆方向」，選左撇子、右撇子或不用筆。

怎麼解除封鎖？

到「設定 → 封鎖名單」，找到那個人按「解除」。

怎麼刪除我的資料／帳號？

「設定 → 刪除我的資料」。這會刪掉本機所有紀錄，以及雲端的個人檔案、作品、大頭貼與匿名帳號本身。 這個動作無法復原。若你在 App 內操作有困難，寄信給我們也可以代為處理。

需要註冊帳號嗎？

不需要。Drawverse 不要 email、不要密碼、不要姓名。第一次開啟時會自動建立一個匿名編號，就這樣。

我換手機了，作品會跟著過去嗎？

打開「設定 → iCloud 同步」，作品會備份到你自己的 iCloud，在同一個 Apple ID 的裝置上可以取回。

「鄰近模式」連不上？

鄰近模式用本地網路連線，請確認：兩台裝置在同一個 Wi-Fi、藍牙已開啟， 並且在「iOS 設定 → Drawverse → 本地網路」允許權限。

線上房間進不去 / 一直轉圈？

多半是網路問題，請先切換 Wi-Fi／行動網路再試一次。若持續發生，請寄信告訴我們發生的時間與房號。

可以離線玩嗎？

可以。「隨性的畫」不需要網路，「你畫我猜」的鄰近模式也只用本地連線；每日挑戰與線上房間需要連線。

餵怪獸的「加時間」是怎麼算的？

怪獸認出你畫的東西並吃掉時，下一回合就會多給你幾秒鐘，畫面上的沙漏會顯示目前累積到多少秒。 牠沒認出來就不加時間——按「不會畫，跳過」換一題不會被扣分。

FAQ

I saw content that upset me. What do I do?

Open the artwork and tap Report. We review reports within 24 hours, remove violating content and disable the account. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically until we have looked at it. You can also tap Block this person — you will never see their content again.

The button turns into “Report received” only once the server has actually stored it. If it says the report could not be sent, tap again or email ctwu95@gmail.com — the same 24-hour commitment applies.

What is Drawverse Pro?

A one-time in-app purchase (not a subscription). It unlocks five pens — brush, ink brush, chalk, watercolour and rainbow — plus custom colours and the ability to host prank-mode rooms. Joining someone else's prank room does not require the purchase.

I got a new phone. Do I have to buy it again?

No. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Settings → Drawverse Pro → Restore purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple; we cannot issue them. Please file a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I write my own topic?

Yes. Submit one in the guessing-game lobby and it is used for the round where you draw first. Before that round everyone else only sees that you have submitted something, not what it says. Players who do not submit get an AI topic for their round.

The author is named at the reveal. The seat that draws immediately before a correct guess scores the most, and that seat is the author — naming them simply makes it visible when someone writes an easy topic for themselves.

What are the faces that appear while I am drawing?

Live reactions tapped by the people watching. They float past and are not stored anywhere.

How does the signature feature work?

When you finish a drawing in free drawing or the daily challenge, Drawverse asks whether you want to sign it. Pick one of the built-in styles (script, initials, handwriting, rounded, brush, mark) or draw your own — a hand-drawn signature lets you choose the pen and the colour. Then drag it anywhere on the canvas you could draw, and pinch or rotate to size it.

Your signature is stored only on your device and is never uploaded, unless you stamp it onto a drawing you publish. Change or delete it under Settings → My signature, and turn off “ask me when I finish” if you do not want the prompt.

Who writes the comments and scores?

An AI does. Daily-challenge comments and scores, feed-the-monster and COPYCAT recognition, topic generation and the AI sample artwork all send your drawing to a third-party AI service — see section 4 of the Privacy Policy. The AI misreads drawings and makes things up, so treat the scores as entertainment.

The pen on the canvas points the wrong way

Go to Settings → Pen direction and choose left-handed, right-handed, or no pen.

How do I unblock someone?

Go to Settings → Blocked list and tap Unblock.

How do I delete my data / account?

Settings → Delete my data. This removes all local records plus your cloud profile, artwork, avatar and the anonymous account itself. It cannot be undone. Email us if you need help.

Do I need an account?

No. Drawverse asks for no email, password or name. An anonymous ID is created automatically on first launch.

I got a new phone — do my drawings follow me?

Turn on Settings → iCloud sync. Artwork is backed up to your own iCloud and restored on devices sharing your Apple ID.

Nearby play won’t connect

Nearby play uses the local network. Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, Bluetooth is on, and Local Network permission is allowed in iOS Settings → Drawverse.

Can I play offline?

Yes — free drawing works offline, and nearby guessing games run entirely over the local network. The daily challenge and online rooms need a connection.

How does the extra time in feed-the-monster work?

Every time the monster recognises and eats what you drew, you get a few more seconds in the next round; the hourglass on screen shows how much you have banked. If it does not recognise the drawing you get nothing extra — and tapping skip for a new prompt costs you nothing.
